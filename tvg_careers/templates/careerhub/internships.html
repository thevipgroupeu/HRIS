INTERNSHIPS AT THEVIPGROUP

Learn inside real work.

Our internships connect a defined learning plan with practical product, engineering, operations or people challenges. You contribute; you are not left to improvise without context.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Structured enough to learn. Practical enough to matter.

Each listing sets out responsibilities, learning outcomes, expected dates, duration and working mode. A named supervisor or hiring manager remains accountable inside PeopleOps.

01

A defined brief

You start with a clear problem, intended outcome and boundaries appropriate to your experience.

02

Regular feedback

Your supervisor reviews progress, helps remove blockers and makes expectations explicit.

03

Documented learning

You can connect work completed to skills developed and university learning outcomes.

04

Human decisions

People review applications and make decisions. Automated systems do not hire or reject candidates.

WHO CAN APPLY

Students, graduates and emerging professionals.

Eligibility differs by role. Some placements are designed for active university students, some welcome recent graduates, and some can support Erasmus+ traineeship arrangements. The individual listing is always the source of truth.

We value evidence of curiosity, care, reliability and the ability to explain your thinking. A long list of credentials is not a substitute for a thoughtful application.
