RECRUITMENT PROCESS

Clear steps. Human decisions.

We aim to make the process proportionate to the role and transparent about what happens next. No AI system makes hiring or rejection decisions.

  1. 01

    Application

    Create or use your secure candidate account, review the role and submit the requested information for that specific opportunity.

  2. 02

    Human review

    The recruitment team reviews the application against the published role. Your other applications remain independent.

  3. 03

    Conversation or task

    Depending on the role, we may invite you to a structured conversation or a relevant, time-bounded exercise.

  4. 04

    Decision and planning

    We communicate the outcome and, where relevant, confirm supervision, dates and internship documentation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Useful answers before you apply.

Can I apply to more than one role?

Yes. Each application is independent and is reviewed within its own recruitment process.

Are all internships eligible for Erasmus+?

No. Use the Erasmus+ filter and check the individual role. Your university must separately confirm programme and funding eligibility.

Do you offer remote internships?

Working mode is stated on every listing. Some work requires time in Kraków or with a specific team even when a role is hybrid.

Does AI review or reject my application?

No. Automated tools may support administration, but they do not make or enact hiring or rejection decisions.

Where is candidate information stored?

Submitted applications are handled in THEVIPGROUP PeopleOps, our self-hosted HR system of record. Public job pages never expose candidate or employee records.

Who can I contact?

Email {{ contact_email }} with the role title and a concise question. Do not email sensitive identity documents unless our team has requested an approved secure method.
