Bring your learning agreement into practical work.

Selected roles may support an Erasmus+ traineeship. Eligibility and funding are determined by your sending institution and programme—not by THEVIPGROUP.

PLAN BEFORE YOU APPLY

Four things to confirm.

Start with your university’s international office. Grant processes and document lead times vary, so an early, accurate conversation helps everyone.

  1. 01

    University eligibility

    Confirm that your programme permits a traineeship in Poland and that your proposed dates are eligible.

  2. 02

    Funding and insurance

    Understand your grant, payment schedule, insurance obligations and personal budget.

  3. 03

    Learning agreement

    Map the role’s published learning outcomes to your institution’s required documentation.

  4. 04

    Dates and supervision

    Agree a feasible duration, start window, working mode and supervisor before documents are signed.

IMPORTANT

An Erasmus+ label is not an offer or funding decision.

Applying does not guarantee selection, a grant, accommodation, immigration status or university approval. We do not automatically decide eligibility and we do not issue documents before the relevant human review.
