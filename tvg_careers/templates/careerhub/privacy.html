About the public catalogue

These pages request a field-limited catalogue of published opportunities from our private PeopleOps service. Public responses contain role information only. Draft opportunities, closed Horilla recruitment records, employee details and candidate information are not returned.

Candidate account information

When you create an account we process your name, personal email, a password hash, email-verification state, privacy-notice acknowledgement and any optional talent-pool preference. We also keep data-minimised security events using keyed fingerprints rather than raw IP addresses. We use this information to provide and protect your requested account and to prepare for recruitment steps you choose to start.

Your password is hashed and is never included in verification or recovery email. Candidate accounts are separate from employee and administrator identities.

Privacy acknowledgement and optional consent

Creating an account requires you to acknowledge that you have read this notice. That acknowledgement is not presented as consent to all recruitment processing. The separate talent-pool contact choice is optional, is not required to create an account and can be changed in your profile.

Profiles, applications and documents

You can store reusable contact, education, language, availability, Erasmus+ self-declaration, CV and cover-letter information. Starting a draft does not submit it. When you choose to submit, we retain a separate application-time copy of your profile, answers, CV and optional cover letter and create the corresponding recruitment record in our private PeopleOps system. Later reusable-profile or document changes do not alter a submitted application.

Application progress is shown using candidate-friendly statuses only. CareerHub does not expose reviewer comments, internal ratings, AI assessments, staff-only notes or other applicants.

Retention and withdrawal

You may withdraw a submitted application from your dashboard. Withdrawal preserves the recruitment record and status history needed to document the process; it does not mean every record is immediately erased. Account, application, document and security records are retained only for applicable recruitment, legal and security periods. Requests for access, correction, restriction or deletion are assessed against continuing legal obligations and security holds.

Operational information

Our infrastructure processes limited request and security information needed to deliver and protect the service. This implementation does not add advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts.
